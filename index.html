GOVERNMENT SOLAR SUBSIDY

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

Eligible residential consumers can receive financial assistance for eligible grid-connected rooftop solar systems under the applicable Central and Uttar Pradesh government schemes.

₹
Up to ₹1,08,000 Potential combined Central Government and Uttar Pradesh Government subsidy for eligible residential rooftop solar systems up to 3 kW.
Solar System Central Subsidy UP State Subsidy Total
1 kW ₹30,000 ₹15,000 ₹45,000
2 kW ₹60,000 ₹30,000 ₹90,000
3 kW ₹78,000 ₹30,000 ₹1,08,000
Above 3 kW ₹78,000 maximum ₹30,000 maximum ₹1,08,000 maximum
🏠

Residential Consumers

The subsidy is intended for eligible residential electricity consumers with an applicable electricity connection and eligible rooftop solar installation.

☀

Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar

PM Surya Ghar subsidy applies to eligible residential grid-connected rooftop solar installations under the applicable scheme requirements.
